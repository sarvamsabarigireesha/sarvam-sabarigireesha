About Lord Ayyappa & This Temple

Lord Ayyappa, the son of Shiva and Mohini (Vishnu's avatar), is revered as Dharma Shasta — the guardian of righteousness. Devotees observe the sacred 41-day Vratham before undertaking the pilgrimage to the eighteen holy steps.

This temple serves as a spiritual home for Ayyappa devotees, offering regular pujas, seva bookings, community gatherings and a direct connection to Sabarimala traditions — wherever devotees may be.

How it works

Monthly Ayyappa Prasadam

Registration is completely free. When prasadam becomes available, the temple opens the lottery and randomly selects winners.

Winners are notified immediately and prasadam is shipped to their address at no cost. Contributions are entirely at each devotee's own wish.

● Lottery: Opens when prasadam is available

Free entry. No purchase necessary. One entry per devotee per month.
