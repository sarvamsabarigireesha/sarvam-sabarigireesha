About THATHWAMASI

A digital sanctum, quietly built in the shadow of the Western Ghats.

Our Name, Our Vow

“Thathwamasi” — Thou art That.

THATHWAMASI is a threshold — a quiet, unhurried place for the devotees of Lord Ayyappa. A place to return to between pilgrimages, to remember why you climb, and to keep the lamp lit when you cannot be at Sannidhanam.

The Journey

Six thresholds, one path to Sannidhanam.

The Sabarimala pilgrimage is one of the great yatras of India — not merely a walk to a shrine, but a deliberate ascent through inner and outer landscape.

01

Vratham

The forty-one day vow. Discipline, restraint, and a slow turning inward.

02

Irumudi

Carrying the Irumudi is a surrender of worldly desires and a balance of mind.

03

Erumeli

The threshold of the forest, where the pilgrim leaves the world behind.

04

Pampa

The sacred river — a bath of clarity before the climb.

05

Pathinettampadi

The eighteen sacred steps, a ledger of inner virtues offered at Ayyappa's feet.

06

Sannidhanam

The sanctum. Karpoora aarti, granite silence, and the recognition — Thathwamasi.
