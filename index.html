About

Sarvam Sabarigireesha — Devotee Community Portal

Sarvam Sabarigireesha is a community portal serving devotees of Lord Ayyappa Swamy, the son of Shiva and Mohini (an avatar of Vishnu), worshipped as Dharma Shasta — protector of righteousness.

This portal provides Darshan and Puja timings, temple announcements, the Sabarimala pilgrimage calendar, and the monthly Prasadam registration process for devotees observing the sacred Vratham.

The portal is community-run and updated regularly. For any correction or query, please use the Contact section below.

How It Works

Monthly Ayyappa Prasadam

Registration is free of cost. When Prasadam becomes available, registration opens and recipients are selected at random from registered devotees.

Selected devotees are notified immediately and Prasadam is shipped to their registered address at no charge. Any donation is entirely optional and at the devotee's discretion.

● Status: Opens when Prasadam is available

Free registration. No purchase necessary. One entry per devotee per month.

Reference

Stages of the Sabarimala Pilgrimage

Stage 1

Vratham

A 41-day period of discipline and abstinence observed before the pilgrimage begins.

Stage 2

Irumudi Kettu

The sacred double-pouch offering carried on the head during the ascent.

Stage 3

Erumeli

The traditional starting point of the forest route to Sabarimala.

Stage 4

Pampa

The river where pilgrims bathe before the final ascent to the temple.

Stage 5

Pathinettampadi

The eighteen sacred steps leading to the sanctum, each representing an inner virtue.

Stage 6

Sannidhanam

The sanctum sanctorum — the destination of the pilgrimage.
