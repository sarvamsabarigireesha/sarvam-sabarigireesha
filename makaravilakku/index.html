Makaravilakku 2027 — Date, Makara Jyothi & Festival Guide

Swamiye Sharanam Ayyappa! Makaravilakku is the crowning festival of the Sabarimala season — the sacred Makara Jyothi darshan on Makara Sankranti day. Here are the verified 2026-27 dates, viewing guidance and booking tips from Sarvam Sabarigireesha (Thathwamasi).

🪔 What is Makaravilakku & Makara Jyothi?

On Makara Sankranti night, devotees witness the Makara Jyothi — the sacred light appearing on the horizon hills around Sannidhanam — while the Makaravilakku (the great lamp) is lit at Ponnambalamedu. For millions of Ayyappa devotees this darshan is the spiritual summit of the 41-day vratham.

🎫 Planning Your Makaravilakku Darshan

  • Virtual Q booking: free, at sabarimalaonline.org — the Makaravilakku window opens with the season booking (~1 November); 14 Jan slots finish fastest
  • Stay: book TDB rooms at onlinetdb.com (Sannidhanam/Pamba) the moment the window opens — see our tickets & accommodation guide
  • Arrive a day early; carry the offline QR coupon, original ID, woolens (night chill) and black/blue attire
  • Jyothi darshan viewing areas around Sannidhanam get crowded by afternoon — follow Kerala Police instructions

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When is Makaravilakku in 2027?

Makaravilakku / Makara Jyothi day falls on 14 January 2027. The season runs from ~30 December 2026 (5 PM opening) to ~20 January 2027.

Is Makara Jyothi the same as Makaravilakku?

They are witnessed together on Makara Sankranti night: the Makaravilakku lamp is lit at Ponnambalamedu while devotees see the Makara Jyothi light on the horizon — both form the festival's sacred darshan.

Do I need a ticket for Makaravilakku darshan?

Yes — a free Virtual Q coupon from sabarimalaonline.org is required for darshan on open days, including 14 January. Book early; it is the busiest day of the season.
