How to Reach Sabarimala — Travel Guide for Telangana & AP Devotees

Planning your yatra from Telangana, Andhra or Tamil Nadu? This guide from Sarvam Sabarigireesha covers trains, buses, flights and the final Pamba → Sannidhanam trek — with season-wise practical tips.

🚆 By Train (best from Telangana/AP)

  • Chengannur (~90 km from Pamba) is the main railhead — the Sabari Express (Secunderabad–Thiruvananthapuram) and many superfast trains stop here
  • During Mandala-Makaravilakku season, special trains run from Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam & Chennai towards Chengannur/Tiruvalla — check the season timetable
  • From Chengannur: KSRTC/APSRTC buses and shared jeeps to Pamba (~2.5-3 hrs via Ranni)

🚌 By Bus

  • APSRTC / TSRTC: seasonal direct specials to Pamba from Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Guntur, Tirupati; regular services to Chengannur year-round
  • KSRTC: frequent Pamba chains from Kottayam, Chengannur, Ernakulam & Trivandrum; Pamba is the road-head of the pilgrimage
  • Nilakkal base camp (parking & shuttle) is useful in peak crowd days

✈️ By Air

  • Cochin International (COK) ~160 km or Trivandrum (TRV) ~170 km; then KSRTC/taxi to Pamba (4-5 hrs)

🥾 Pamba → Sannidhanam Trek (~5 km)

  • Traditional route: Pamba Ganapathy temple → Neelimala → Appachimedu → Sannidhanam (steeper, sacred)
  • Gentler route: Swami Ayyappan road via Karimala bypass for elders — medical/attendant access available
  • Start early morning; carry water, offline Virtual Q coupon, original ID; follow the vratham dress code
  • Stay options: TDB rooms at Sannidhanam/Pamba via onlinetdb.com — see accommodation guide

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the nearest railway station to Sabarimala?

Chengannur (~90 km from Pamba) is the main railhead; Sabari Express and seasonal special trains connect it with Hyderabad, Vijayawada and Chennai.

How far is the Pamba to Sannidhanam trek?

About 5 km via the traditional Neelimala-Appachimedu route; a gentler alternative road exists for elderly devotees.

Are there direct buses from Hyderabad to Sabarimala?

Yes — APSRTC/TSRTC run seasonal special buses to Pamba during Mandala-Makaravilakku; year-round you can bus to Chengannur and switch to KSRTC/jeep to Pamba.
