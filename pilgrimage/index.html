🎫 Sabarimala Virtual Queue Tickets (Virtual Q) — Free Darshan Booking

The Sabarimala virtual queue ticket is FREE — it is a darshan slot coupon issued by TDB, not a paid ticket. Booking is mandatory for darshan on open days.

  1. Go to the official portal: sabarimalaonline.org (virtualq.sabarimalaonline.org) or sabarimala.kerala.gov.in
  2. Register with mobile + email (new users) or log in
  3. Choose your darshan date from the open calendar
  4. Add devotee details — one adult (18+) per group; carry the same original ID (Aadhaar / Voter / Passport)
  5. Confirm and download the QR coupon offline before your journey
  • Booking window: opens 10-15 days before each opening; Mandala-Makaravilakku booking typically opens ~1 November
  • Insurance: Virtual Q bookers get accidental-death cover (₹5 lakh) at no premium
  • Children below 5: no ticket required
  • Spot booking: if slots are full, counters at Pamba, Nilakkal & Chengannur issue QR passes

🏨 TDB Accommodation — Rooms at Sannidhanam, Pamba & Nilakkal

All Sabarimala accommodation is managed by the Travancore Devaswom Board and booked only at onlinetdb.com. Sannidhanam (hilltop, near the temple) has around 540 rooms across buildings including Sabari Guest House, plus cottages; Pamba and Nilakkal base camps offer additional rooms and dormitories.

  1. Open onlinetdb.com → Accommodation section
  2. Register / log in with mobile number
  3. Select zone (Sannidhanam / Pamba / Nilakkal), dates & room type
  4. Pay online and save the booking confirmation offline
  • Rooms open in limited windows before each season — book the moment booking opens
  • Vazhipadu offerings (Neyyabhishekam, Padi Pooja, Udayasthamana Pooja…) can be booked with your Virtual Q ticket at sabarimalaonline.org

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are Sabarimala virtual queue tickets free?

Yes — the Virtual Q darshan slot is completely free. Book only at sabarimalaonline.org or sabarimala.kerala.gov.in.

When does booking open for Mandala-Makaravilakku 2026-27?

Generally 10-15 days before each opening; the big season window typically opens around 1 November 2026. Book immediately for 27 Dec (Mandala Pooja) and 14 Jan (Makara Jyothi).

Where do I book TDB accommodation?

Only at onlinetdb.com — the official Travancore Devaswom Board portal for Sannidhanam, Pamba and Nilakkal rooms.

What ID proofs are needed?

Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport for 18+; school or college ID for ages 5-18; below 5 years no ticket needed. Carry the original ID used at booking.
