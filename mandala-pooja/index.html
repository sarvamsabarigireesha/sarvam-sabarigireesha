Mandala Pooja 2026 — Date & 41-Day Mandala Kalam Guide

The Mandala Kalam is the heart of the Sabarimala season — 41 days when lakhs of mala-wearing devotees climb to Sannidhanam and conclude their vratham with Mandala Pooja. Verified 2026 dates and planning guidance from Sarvam Sabarigireesha.

🛕 What is Mandala Pooja?

Mandala Pooja concludes the 41-day Mandala Kalam (Vrischikam month onwards). Devotees who wore the Ayyappa mala complete their vratham with darshan during this season — the hill vibrates with "Swamiye Sharanam Ayyappa" from dawn to night. It is one of the two great festivals of Sabarimala, alongside Makaravilakku.

🎫 Darshan Planning & Crowd Tips

  • Virtual Q: free coupon at sabarimalaonline.org; Mandala-season booking opens ~1 November — weekends & December days fill fastest
  • Weekday darshan (Nov 17 – Dec 10) has visibly lighter crowds than Mandala Pooja week
  • Stay: TDB rooms via onlinetdb.com; Pamba/Nilakkal base camps as backup — full steps in our accommodation guide
  • Finish your 41-day vratham before darshan; carry irumudi, original ID, offline coupon

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When is Mandala Pooja 2026?

Mandala Pooja falls on 27 December 2026, concluding the Mandala Kalam that opens on 16 November 2026.

How long is the Mandala season at Sabarimala?

About 41 days — from 16 November 2026 to 27 December 2026 — after which the temple closes briefly and reopens on 30 December for Makaravilakku.

When should I complete my Ayyappa vratham?

Traditionally devotees complete the 41-day vratham with Sabarimala darshan during Mandala Kalam or Makaravilakku season; see our vratham guide for the full ritual sequence.
