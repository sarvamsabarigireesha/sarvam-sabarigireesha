Ayyappa Mala Deeksha & 41 Days Vratham — Complete Guide

Wearing the Ayyappa mala and observing the 41-day vratham is the soul of the Sabarimala pilgrimage. This guide from Sarvam Sabarigireesha (Thathwamasi) covers deeksha, daily discipline, irumudi kettu and mala removal — for first-time devotees and seasoned swamis alike.

📿 Taking Deeksha (Wearing the Mala)

  • Choose a guru (an elder devotee/swami) and take the mala at a temple or your home altar, ideally 41+ days before your darshan date
  • The mala (rudraksha/beads) is worn after purification; from that day you are addressed as Ayyappa/Swami
  • Set your darshan date early and book the free Virtual Q slot — see our booking guide

🙏 41-Day Vratham Rules

  • Food: strict vegetarian; no alcohol, tobacco or intoxicants
  • Dress: black, blue or saffron clothes; footwear minimal, head uncovered at shrines
  • Discipline: celibacy, sleep on the floor where possible, daily bath before prayers
  • Practice: daily bhajan/prayer, temple visits, chanting Swamiye Sharanam Ayyappa; greet fellow devotees with the same mantra
  • Conduct: truthfulness, patience, service to fellow swamis — the vratham is as much inner as outer

🎒 Irumudi Kettu — What Goes Inside

  • Front compartment (offering): coconut filled with ghee (neyy), rice, jaggery, aval (flattened rice), coconuts for abhishekam, incense, sandal paste
  • Back compartment (personal): clothes, toiletries, medicines, snack items for the trek
  • Carry the irumudi on your head during the Pamba–Sannidhanam climb — see our travel guide for routes

🕉️ After Darshan — Mala Removal

After Sannidhanam darshan, devotees remove the mala at the temple vicinity or upon returning home before their family altar, keeping the mala beads safely for the next season. Many devotees observe a simple thanksgiving meal (anna-daanam) sharing Swami's prasadam.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many days is the Ayyappa vratham?

Traditionally 41 days of austerity before darshan; devotees with less time observe a minimum of one mandala (or as guided by their guru) — the discipline matters more than the count.

Can women or first-time devotees wear the mala?

Sabarimala tradition follows the temple's current legal and ritual guidelines; first-time male devotees of all ages wear the mala after deeksha. Check the latest TDB advisory before planning.

What if I break a vratham rule by accident?

Devotees traditionally observe prayaschittam (a simple corrective vow) as guided by their guru and continue; the vratham's spirit is sincerity, not perfection.
